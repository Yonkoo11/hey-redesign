What it is for

$HEY coordinates research participation around HEY Research Lab. It is working capital for evidence work, and it never buys ranking or positive coverage. Eight things it does, each switched on as its integration is verified on chain:

Each is switched on as its integration is verified on chain.

  • Pay for research and placementsRequest Research and paid placements are priced in HEY, lower for accounts that hold HEY.
  • Earn research bountiesEvidence work the treasury pays for: half at approval, half ninety days later if the evidence still stands.
  • Back a claim with a bondA Scout bonds to claim a bounty; an owner may bond an update; a submission may carry one. Returned when the claim checks out.
  • Read with an API keyA key reads the same public data with a monthly allowance that follows your tier.
  • Read research firstNew research notes open to holding tiers before they open to everyone.
  • Vote on research fundingOnce a month, say where the treasury’s research third goes. Advisory; the founder decides and publishes.
  • One wallet, linked onceEvery payment, payout and tier comes from the one wallet linked to your account by signature.
  • A ledger, monthlyEvery HEY that moves is a row with its transaction; the month’s table is published here.

How it launched

$HEY launched on Pons V2 on 9 September 2026: a bonding curve paired with ETH, one launch configuration, no presale. The curve opened at a 1.68 ETH valuation and graduated once 4.2 ETH had been raised; the raised ETH and the reserved 28.6% of supply seeded a full-range Uniswap v4 pool that Pons holds permanently. The figures below are the Pons V2 launch configuration HEY recorded on 9 September 2026 and the contract HEY verified at launch; they are not re-read from the chain on every visit.

Research funding

The treasury’s research third pays for evidence work through research bounties: half at approval, half ninety days later if the evidence still stands. Rewards are set in dollars by tier and quoted in HEY once, from HEY’s own price reading, when a bounty is posted; treasury bounties in any month are capped at a share of the treasury. Every payout is a transfer the founder sends and the worker verifies; the ledger is published monthly.

Where the creator tax goes

2% of every trade goes to the HEY treasury and is spent in three equal parts.

Research bounties1/3Pays researchers and builders who verify ships, correct records and map sources
Infrastructure and data1/3Servers, RPC, indexing and the public API
Platform operations1/3Moderation, the weekly content, badges and films

No buyback, no market operations. A ledger of what came in and what was spent is published here every month; the first entry is due on 9 October 2026.
