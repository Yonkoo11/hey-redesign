Is anyone building this?

Paste a Robinhood Chain contract address. HEY reads the chain, the explorer and whatever the token declares about itself, and answers that one question with the source behind every line.

It is not a risk check. Other tools read holders and wallets to judge what a token might do next; HEY reads neither, and this is meant to sit beside them rather than replace them.
