Methodology

Scoring version hbm-v5. Every rule below is deterministic: the same evidence always produces the same result.

What counts as building

HEY records a ShipEvent when a project does something concrete: a release, a deployment, an integration, an SDK, documentation, or, for meme projects, a game, tool, bot, art drop or community event. Creative and community output is real output. A meme is never required to pretend it ships software.

Announcements, hype, giveaways and marketing posts are recorded but never counted as building. An event also has to be backed by a public source or better before it can affect a project's status, so a project cannot talk its way into looking active.

Activity is never derived from token price, market cap, trading volume, holders or wallets.

Discovery Gap and Still Building

Discovery Gap compares how actively a project ships against its current market footprint. A project appears Under the Radar when it is active, has Build Momentum of at least 30, and has at least 2 source-backed updates in the last 30 days.

Still Building is shown when a project's market value is at or below 50% of the high HEY itself observed in the last 90 days, and meaningful building continued after that decline began.

Under the Radar
active · Build Momentum ≥ 30 · 2 updates in 30 days
Still Building
≤ 50% of the 90-day high · building continued

These are factual observations about past activity and market context. They are not predictions, not valuations, and not investment advice. HEY never claims that continued building will affect a token's price.

Token market status and token verification

A project can be building while its token has no market, and a token can trade while nobody builds. HEY keeps the two apart. Activity status reads ships and repositories. Token market status reads HEY's own market snapshots and is shown beside it, never inside it: Active market, Low liquidity (under $5K), No liquidity, Trading inactive (liquidity but no trades in 24 hours), Liquidity no longer detected (HEY recorded at least $5K earlier and now sees a tenth of it or less), Market not detected (no reading for 30 days after a market existed), or Market data insufficient. These are observations HEY made; none is a verdict on why.

Token verification asks a different question: is the token HEY tracks the project's own? Finding a token through a launchpad record, a market listing or a matching name establishes nothing. A token is Verified only when the project itself ties the contract to the project: its own site names the address, or the owner proves control of the deployer with an on-chain signature. Contract mismatch means the project's site names a different contract than the one tracked. Everything else stays Unverified, with the reason on the page.

The links on a project page carry their own, separate label, and it is not the same question as either of the above.

Verified
Verified means HEY confirmed the project controls that link.
Official
Official means HEY found it published on the project's own domain, which is evidence rather than a claim.
Declared by the project
Declared by the project means someone put their name to it on a submission and a moderator approved the page, a claim with a person behind it.
Unverified
Unverified means HEY found the link and nobody has stood behind it. A project can be a verified builder while its links are only declared: the badge is about what was shipped, the label is about who says the link is theirs.

Two consequences. Still Building needs a market to decline against, so a token with no liquidity, a removed market or a launch pool nobody traded never carries the badge. And the home page lists projects whose tracked token still has a market, or no token at all; the rest keep their page and their place on Explore, with the fact stated. Neither changes activity status or Build Momentum.

Evidence

Every update keeps its source. HEY distinguishes what a project claims from what can be checked:

○self reported
means a builder told us
◐source linked
means it came from a source registered to that project
●source verified
means HEY confirmed it against a public source such as a GitHub release

Disputed and retracted updates keep their record but stop counting.

Reading HEY as data

Everything on these pages is also readable as JSON, with no key and no account:

/api/projects
for the catalogue
/api/projects/{slug}
for one project
/api/ships
for what shipped

The same filters as the browse pages, the same counts, and every fact carries the source behind it. Anything HEY does not know is left out rather than sent as a zero. For a reader rather than a script, the same material is published as RSS.

What HEY does not do

HEY does not track wallets or holders, and stores no wallet, holder, PnL or smart-money data of any kind. It does not rank by price, and organic placement cannot be bought.
